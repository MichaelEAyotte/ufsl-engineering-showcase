# Contributing

Submit only material you own or are authorized to contribute. Describe its provenance, identify any third-party or client-owned material and its applicable terms, and disclose AI assistance and any known limits on ownership or protectability. Do not include private records, credentials or material whose rights are unresolved.

Contributions to this distribution must be offered under the same [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 license](LICENSE), for the rights you are authorized to grant. Its permitted purposes and organizations are defined by the official text. Preserve applicable notices and follow the [scope in NOTICE](NOTICE). This is a source-available project, not OSI-approved open source.

Contributors retain their copyright. Contributing does not automatically assign copyright or grant Michael E. Ayotte permission to relicense contributor-owned material commercially. Separate written rights must be obtained from the relevant rights holder before such material can be included in a separate commercial license. Contact [Michael E. Ayotte](https://github.com/MichaelEAyotte) to discuss a proposed contribution or separate permission.

Explain the change, its scope and how you checked it. For code changes, run `npm test` and `npm run demo`; use fictional examples and keep the standalone module independent of production data and deployment systems. Check documentation links for documentation changes.

[Earlier MIT grants continue](PUBLICATION.md#earlier-mit-publication); these contribution terms do not revoke them or claim ownership of unprotectable material.
